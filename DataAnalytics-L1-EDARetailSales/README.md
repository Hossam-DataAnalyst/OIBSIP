Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis

Objective:
The goal of this project is to perform a comprehensive EDA on a retail sales dataset to uncover patterns and actionable business insights.

Tech Stack:
Python, pandas, matplotlib, seaborn.

Key Insights:
1- May 2023 recorded the highest sales volume.
2- January 2024 shows a sharp data drop, indicating incomplete data.
3- Gender distribution is nearly equal (51% Female, 49% Male). The 60-64 age group is a massive purchasing segment.
4- Electronics and Clothing are top revenue drivers, outperforming Beauty.

Actionable Recommendations:

Direct marketing budget to the 60-64 age group.

Introduce cross-selling bundle offers for Electronics and Clothing.

Investigate data pipeline for January 2024 to rectify missing records.
